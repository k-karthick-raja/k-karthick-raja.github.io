    About

    From Android Java
    to Flutter at scale.

    A developer who has shipped consumer apps, worked on high-scale fintech, and spent time on the security side analysing Android apps.

    I started with native Android in Java — APIs with Retrofit and Volley, Firebase, Maps and push notifications — and moved into Flutter as cross-platform became the way serious product teams ship. Today I lead Flutter work on a large-scale banking app, write unit tests for Cubits, review code, and guide junior developers.

    Between product roles, I worked as an Android malware analyst, reviewing apps at volume for spyware, toll fraud and maskware. That security grounding still shapes how I build: careful with permissions, data and edge cases.

    Outside day-to-day product work, I'm building the AI Finance Coach in public on GitHub — a personal-finance app planned around spending insights, low-balance alerts and an EMI & bill planner, on demo data.

    I'm also levelling up in Python, data structures & algorithms, and machine learning — the foundations behind the coaching features I want the app to grow into.
